3. Children

Under 13 is not allowed. A new account cannot be created if the date of birth is under 13.

Ages 13 to 17 are allowed in the pilot as supervised accounts. A parent or guardian (18 or older) links the account and consents, approves joins and chats, and can pause or delete the account. See the Parent / Guardian Consent and the Parent and guardian privacy notice. We collect only what the service needs. Minors' share cards are followers-only and their date of birth is never shown. Supervision ends on the 18th birthday.

An account that already existed below 13 is not deleted by this rule. It stays, and it stays supervised, until the person turns 18. A person under 13 still cannot open an account.

A guest join (name and mobile number, no full account) is only for ages 18 and up. Ages 13 to 17 use a full supervised account instead.

4. Leagues

An organizer creates and runs a league (a season with teams, a schedule, and results). The organizer is responsible for the league’s data. The organizer is the personal information controller of league participant data. SanLaro is only an intermediary utility: it stores the files for the organizer and does not decide the league’s consent process. SanLaro does not hold the bond or any other league money. The organizer collects that.

A league can store:

  • Team names, an optional moniker, optional “represents” text (a barangay, school, church, company, or anything the

organizer types), and roster names. A junior roster can be a name only. That name is not a SanLaro account.

  • Match results.
  • A photo of a signed consent form for a minor. League consent is that photo. A parent account linked on SanLaro

does not replace it.

Consent photos are private. They are not on the public league page (that page shows the table and the fixtures only). The people who can see a photo are only the people the league allows:

  • The league owner and the admins the organizer names.
  • That team’s captain, only for their own team, and only if the organizer turns this on. It stays off until the

organizer turns it on.

The organizer can download one photo, or all of them. From the league end date, the app reminds the organizer to download first. A daily job then deletes the photos and the consent records a set number of days after the league ends. The default is 7 days. The organizer can set a different number, from 1 to 365.

While the league is open, the organizer-only audit line for an upload can include the player’s name. When the photos are deleted, those lines are rewritten so they do not contain player names. The deletion note itself does not name anyone.

The short note shown in the app next to these photos says the legal wording is still pending legal review. This section is that draft. A printable form the organizer can adapt is in league-consent-form-template.md. It is not a finished notice.

5. Who sees what

Your privacy settings control your profile, records and groups. Group admins see what their group needs (members, RSVPs, payment status, member forms). A linked guardian sees the supervised player’s groups, upcoming sessions, and chat metadata (who and when), not the text of messages. SanLaro staff access is role-based (founder, support, moderator), limited to what each task needs, and every access is logged.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Processors: Google Firebase (hosting, database, authentication, storage, functions), email and push delivery providers. Data may be stored outside the Philippines (e.g. Singapore region) with safeguards required by RA 10173.

7. Retention

Account data: until you delete your account (plus the grace period). Backups roll over within 30 days. Anonymised match results, safety reports and audit logs are kept for legal and safety reasons. Age-check ID images: deleted after the decision. Data exports: link expires after 7 days. League consent photos and consent records: deleted the number of days after the league ends that the organizer set (default 7, allowed range 1 to 365), as section 4 describes.

8. Your rights (RA 10173, sec. 16–18)

Be informed, access, object, erasure or blocking, damages, data portability, rectification, and to file a complaint with the NPC. Use Profile › Your data (download / delete), Profile › Privacy, or email hello@sanlaro.com.

9. Security

Encryption in transit, access rules on every record, least-privilege staff roles, access logging. We will notify the NPC and affected people of breaches as required (NPC Circular 16-03).

10. Changes

We'll show a notice in the app and ask you to review material changes.
