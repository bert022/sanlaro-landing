Delete your SanLaro account

Pilot version. Review by Atty. Kim is pending.

You can delete your SanLaro account from the app, or by emailing us. There is a grace period (default 14 days, between 7 and 30) before the deletion finishes. You can cancel during that window from the app.

In the SanLaro app

  1. Open SanLaro and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Your data (or Account data).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. Email/password accounts may need to re-enter the password.
  4. Watch for the deletion banner on Home or Profile if you want to cancel before the grace period ends.

By email

Email hello@sanlaro.com from the address on your account. Ask us to delete your SanLaro account, and include the email or phone you use to sign in. We will confirm before the deletion is scheduled.

If you already started a deletion request from the public deletion flow in a SanLaro build, follow the confirmation link in that email.

What is deleted

  • Profile, photo, bio, sports and privacy settings
  • Date of birth and message settings
  • Follows, follow requests and group memberships
  • Member forms (KYC) and uploaded photos/IDs
  • Payment proof photos and upcoming RSVPs
  • Photos you sent in chats; support requests
  • Your sign-in account (email, phone, linked logins)

What is kept

  • Past match results stay in other players’ history as “Deleted player”
  • Messages you sent stay in others’ chats, shown as “Deleted player” (photos removed)
  • Safety reports and the audit log (legal and safety obligations; no profile data)

Backups roll over within 30 days. More detail is in the Privacy Policy. Questions: hello@sanlaro.com.
